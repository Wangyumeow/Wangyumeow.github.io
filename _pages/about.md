## 研究方向

主要研究兴趣聚焦于**日本问题**与**国际治理议题**两大方向：

- **日本问题研究**：日本的国际传播战略、日本国内政治动态、社交媒体与政治动员
- **国际治理议题研究**：数字经济国际治理、环境与国际政治、国际传播话语与国家形象建构

</div>
<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">

## Research Interests

My research focuses on two main areas: **Japanese Studies** and **International Governance**.

- **Japanese Studies**: Japan's international communication strategies, domestic political dynamics, social media and political mobilization
- **International Governance**: Digital economy governance, environment and international politics, discourse and national image construction in global communication
